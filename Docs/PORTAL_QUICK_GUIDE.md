# Simple portal guide

## For shop staff

1. Sign in to the office portal. Use the eye icon if you need to see your password while typing.
2. At the start of the day, enter the cash already in the drawer and open the shop day.
3. After opening, this panel hides to leave more room for billing. Open the Profile menu and choose **Shop day · opening / closing** to see it again.
4. For a walk-in sale, choose the customer and staff member, add services, and enter quantities. A price changed on the bill applies only to that bill.
5. Choose cash or card. For card payments, choose the receiving account. Record only money actually received.
6. Save the order. Use its details to review payment information or print it through the browser print window.
7. Use Collections for daily records and Expenses for shop spending. Older changes may require permission from the owner.
8. At closing, count the cash and use the shop-day panel to record the closing amount.

For a hotel sale, turn on **Hotel billing**, choose the hotel, and enter the services. **Save bill** creates one invoice for that delivery. **Save & print** also opens your browser's print window. You can make further deliveries for the same hotel in the same month.

If a delivery saves but its invoice fails, find the saved delivery and choose **Issue invoice**. Do not enter that delivery again. Once issued, its items and rates cannot be changed. Payments can still be recorded from its invoice details.

## For the shop owner

- Sign in to the admin portal to manage customers, services, prices and staff access.
- Open the **Shop day · opening / closing** section to review the day's cash information.
- Use reports to review sales, payments and outstanding balances.
- Review staff activity notifications and requests for additional access.
- Hotel customer history is separate from walk-in customer history.

There is no customer self-service login in the current application. Staff record purchases for customers.
