# Pearl Laundry portal — simple user guide

This guide explains what customers, counter staff, and the shop administrator
can do. It uses simple words and does not require computer knowledge.

## The two screens

- **Office portal** is used at the laundry counter. Staff use it to take orders,
  receive payments, print bills, record expenses, and check daily collections.
- **Admin portal** is used by the shop owner or manager. It can be opened on a
  computer, tablet, or mobile phone.

Both screens can be used in English or Arabic.

## What a customer can do

A customer does not need to sign in. Counter staff complete the work for the
customer.

The customer can:

- Give their name and mobile number when placing an order.
- Ask staff to use saved customer details for a faster order.
- Choose to pay by cash or card.
- Pay the full amount or make a partial payment.
- Receive a bill with a clear order token, item list, VAT, payment status,
  balance, and VAT QR code.
- Use the token when asking about an order or requesting another copy of a bill.
- Return later to pay an unpaid balance.

The customer should keep the token because it is the easiest way to find the
order.

## What counter staff can do

### Sign in and password help

Staff sign in to the Office portal with their own username and password. The
eye button shows or hides the password. If a password is forgotten, **Reset
password** sends a request to the shop administrator.

### Take a new order

1. Open **Sales**.
2. Search for the customer by name or mobile number. If the customer is new,
   enter their name and mobile number.
3. Choose the staff member handling the bill.
4. Press **Done** to close the customer and staff section and make more room.
5. Choose a category, then press the large service buttons to add items.
6. Change quantities when needed.
7. Choose **Card** or **Cash**.
8. Enter the received amount and check the balance.
9. Press **Save**, or press **Save & print** to save and print immediately.

Each saved order receives a unique token. Recent orders appear as cards below
the billing area.

### Find, view, update, and print an order

Use the search box at the top. You can search by:

- Order token
- Invoice number
- Customer name
- Customer mobile number

Press a search result or saved-order card to see full details. The detail screen
shows the customer, staff member, dates, every item, totals, payment details,
notes, and activity history.

Staff can update payment information, settlement amounts, bank account, and
notes when allowed. For an unpaid bill changed to Card, **Card amount** is filled
with the full bill amount automatically. Staff can edit it for a partial
payment. Press **Print bill** to print another copy.

### Record an expense

Open **Expenses** to see today's expenses. Press **Add expense**, then enter the
date, category, description, amount, payment method, vendor, and receipt
reference. Save the form when finished.

### Check daily collections

Open **Collections** to see:

- Total sales
- Amount collected
- Amount still unpaid
- Payment method and bank account
- Each order's total, collected amount, and balance

Staff can open order details or correct today's payment. **Cancel order** removes
an incorrect order from collection totals but keeps its history. Older records
may require permission from the administrator.

## What the shop administrator can do

### Receive clear staff notifications

The bell at the top of the Admin portal shows new staff activity. Notifications
include the staff name, order token or expense reference, amount, payment
change, and time. Examples include:

- A new order was created.
- An unpaid or partial bill was updated.
- An order was cancelled.
- An expense was added or changed.
- Staff requested permission for an older record.
- Staff requested a password reset.

The number on the bell shows unread notifications. Open a notification to go to
the related area. Use **Mark all read** after reviewing all items.

### View the dashboard and reports

The dashboard shows sales, collected money, expenses, outstanding balances,
and recent orders. Reports can be filtered by date, customer, paid status,
payment method, and bank account. Reports can also be exported when needed.

### Manage services and prices

The administrator can add, edit, activate, or deactivate:

- Service categories
- Laundry items
- Item prices

Items in the same category use the same category color on the Office portal.

### Manage customers

The administrator can add or update customer details and open a customer's
purchase history.

### Manage expenses and staff

The administrator can review expenses, manage staff accounts and staff details,
review staff-related company debts, and handle password-reset requests.

### Approve older work

When staff need an older collection or need to change an older order or expense,
the administrator receives a request. Approval is only for the exact task and
record shown in the request.

### Set shop and bill details

The administrator can set the shop name, Arabic shop name, address, phone,
email, VAT number, invoice prefix, and bill footer. These details appear on new
bills.

### Import data

The administrator can import prepared CSV files for supported business data.
Always use the sample column order, check required fields, avoid duplicate
records, and test a small file before importing a large file.

## Printing a clear bill

Use an 80 mm receipt roll. In the browser print window:

- Choose 100% scale.
- Use zero margins.
- Turn off browser headers and footers.
- Choose the printer's high-quality or high-density mode.

Only the bill is printed. The token is shown in a clear outlined box, and the
VAT QR code is included on every bill.

## Good daily practice

- Each staff member should use only their own account.
- Never share passwords.
- Check customer, item, payment, and staff details before saving.
- Use the order token when discussing a bill.
- Review unpaid balances and notifications every day.
- Use **Cancel order** only for an incorrect order; do not use it for a normal
  unpaid order.

## Hotel invoices and the public website

Each hotel delivery now has its own A4 invoice. Choose Save bill or Save & print. If a delivery is saved but invoicing fails, use Issue invoice on that saved delivery instead of entering it twice. Earlier invoices remain available in hotel history.

Customers can browse services and contact the shop on the separate public website. They do not have a customer login. Office and admin are separate websites and can stay open in different tabs.
